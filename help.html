Need a little help?

Quick answers about the current project website.

Is the Android app available?

Not yet. The app is in the planning and development roadmap. An official download link will be published here when a tested release is ready.

Can I create an account or buy Premium?

Account, membership and payment features are not active. Any related buttons or sections will be marked Coming Soon until the real service is ready.

Does the AI Studio work now?

The AI Studio section is a visual concept preview. AI generation and project-connected editing are planned for a future app release.

Will it support 4K and 120 FPS?

These are product goals, not guaranteed features. Export options will depend on device hardware, codecs and software support, and will be confirmed through testing.

How can I follow updates?

Follow the official YouTube and Telegram links on the home page for project updates.

How do I report a website issue?

Send a message through the official Telegram channel. Do not send passwords, payment details or other sensitive information.
